# Data Sources

Every source We The People uses, organized by what you're trying to find.

---

## Federal legislation

### Congress.gov
- **URL:** https://www.congress.gov
- **API:** https://api.congress.gov (free, requires `api.data.gov` key)
- **What it has:** Every federal bill and resolution, sponsors, co-sponsors, committee assignments, votes, full bill text, Congressional Record entries, member profiles.
- **Use for:** Bill identification, full text, sponsors, co-sponsors, votes, member bios.

### GovTrack
- **URL:** https://www.govtrack.us
- **What it has:** Plain-language bill summaries, prognosis scores, voting record visualizations, member ideology scores. More analytical than Congress.gov.
- **Use for:** Plain-language summaries, contextualizing votes, finding related bills.

---

## State legislation

### LegiScan
- **URL:** https://legiscan.com
- **What it has:** Bills from all 50 states, plus Puerto Rico, DC, US Congress. Full text, sponsors, status tracking.
- **Use for:** State-level bill research where Congress.gov doesn't apply.

### Ballotpedia
- **URL:** https://ballotpedia.org
- **What it has:** State and local elected officials, ballot measures, state legislation summaries, election results.
- **Use for:** State legislator bios, ballot measure research, local elected officials.

### Open States
- **URL:** https://openstates.org
- **API:** https://docs.openstates.org
- **What it has:** State legislation, legislator profiles, committee assignments. Open data.
- **Use for:** Programmatic state legislative research.

---

## Campaign finance

### OpenSecrets
- **URL:** https://www.opensecrets.org
- **What it has:** Federal campaign finance, member-by-member donor lists, industry breakdowns, PAC data, lobbying.
- **Use for:** Top donors, industry breakdowns, dark money tracking, PAC contributions.

### FEC.gov
- **URL:** https://www.fec.gov
- **API:** https://api.open.fec.gov (free, requires `api.data.gov` key)
- **What it has:** Raw federal campaign finance filings. candidates, committees, individual contributions, expenditures, PAC activity.
- **Use for:** Authoritative FEC data, raw contribution records, committee summaries.

### FollowTheMoney
- **URL:** https://www.followthemoney.org
- **What it has:** State-level campaign finance, judicial races, ballot measures.
- **Use for:** State legislators (FEC and OpenSecrets are federal-only).

## Financial disclosures

### Unusual Whales
- **URL:** https://unusualwhales.com/politicians
- **What it has:** Aggregated congressional stock trades from periodic transaction reports (PTRs), with timing analysis.
- **Use for:** Quick check of congressional stock trades. Easier than raw filings.

### House disclosure portal
- **URL:** https://disclosures.house.gov
- **What it has:** Annual financial disclosures and PTRs for House members.
- **Use for:** Authoritative disclosure source for House members.

### Senate disclosure portal
- **URL:** https://efts.senate.gov/LATEST/search-index
- **What it has:** Annual financial disclosures and PTRs for Senate members.
- **Use for:** Authoritative disclosure source for Senators.

### House Stock Watcher
- **URL:** https://housestockwatcher.com
- **What it has:** Cleaned-up House PTRs in CSV/JSON format.
- **Use for:** Programmatic analysis of House stock trades.

## Biography and voting record

### VoteSmart
- **URL:** https://votesmart.org
- **What it has:** Legislator bios, voting records, issue positions, public statements, ratings from interest groups.
- **Use for:** Quick biographical lookup, interest group ratings.

### Ballotpedia
Same source as state legislation. Member pages are often the best one-stop summary.

## Lobbying

### Senate Lobbying Disclosure
- **URL:** https://www.lobbyingdisclosure.senate.gov
- **API:** https://lda.congress.gov/api/v1/
- **What it has:** Federal lobbying disclosures. registrants, clients, issues, amounts.
- **Use for:** Identifying who is paying lobbyists to push a bill.

### OpenSecrets Lobbying
- **URL:** https://www.opensecrets.org/lobby
- **What it has:** Same data as the Senate LDA, plus aggregation by industry, client, and bill.
- **Use for:** Easier interface for lobbying-by-industry research.

## Court records

### CourtListener
- **URL:** https://www.courtlistener.com
- **What it has:** Federal and state court opinions, RECAP archive of PACER filings, oral argument audio.
- **Use for:** Litigation history, legal challenges to similar laws.

### Justia
- **URL:** https://www.justia.com
- **What it has:** Free legal opinions, statutes, regulations.
- **Use for:** Reading actual court opinions.

## Source priority cheat sheet

| Question | Go to first |
|---|---|
| What does this bill do? | Congress.gov > GovTrack |
| Who sponsored it? | Congress.gov |
| Who funded the sponsor? | OpenSecrets > FEC API |
| Did the sponsor trade related stocks? | Unusual Whales > House/Senate disclosure portals |
| Who's lobbying for/against? | Senate LDA > OpenSecrets Lobbying |
| Has this bill come up before? | Congress.gov + GovTrack search |
| What's the public saying? | Gallup > Pew > YouGov |
| What's the press saying? | AP/Reuters > Politico > ProPublica |
| Did the sponsor talk about it? | C-SPAN > Listen Notes > official press releases |
| Who benefits financially? | Bill text > cross-reference donors |
